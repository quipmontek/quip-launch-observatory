RESEARCH NOTES / MODEL 1.0

An inspectable valuation, not a target.

1. Select an honest universe

All official watchlist slugs are included. Additional rows require no recorded symbol, CoinGecko ID or positive market cap. Bridge, staking, wrapper, exchange and version-marked products are conservatively screened out. Parent relationships are absent; residual product misclassification is possible.

2. Observe public-market peers

Peers must record a token, positive market cap and TVL, and pass the same product screen. Use the exact category with at least 4 peers; otherwise the related family with at least 6; otherwise the global eligible pool. These are analogues, not economic equivalents. Every detail includes the full selected pool.

3. Robust ratios

Compute each peer's market cap / TVL. Winsorize at interpolated 10th and 90th percentiles; take the median for base, 25th and 75th percentiles for sensitivity. Outliers remain visible in peer tables. Shared token market caps, overlapping TVL and different circulating supplies can bias comparisons.

TVL × peer median
× growth × tracking maturity

Growth = 1 + 0.2 × clamp(7D change, −50%, +50%). Missing growth is neutral, not observed zero. Tracking maturity = 0.70 + 0.30 × min(days tracked / 1,095, 1); missing date uses 0.85. Listing is not launch age or proof of security.

Bear uses peer Q25 × 0.75; bull uses Q75 × 1.25, with the same growth and maturity factors. These are sensitivity scenarios, not statistical bounds. The extra ±25% is an explicit analyst assumption.

Evidence, not certainty

Confidence is Moderate only for exact-category pools of 8+ peers with known age and growth; otherwise Low. Risk lenses flag fallback/missing evidence first, then >20% absolute growth or trading exposure. “Standard caution” never means safe. No protocol has verified tokenomics, fee capture, liquidity or launch intent in this input.
